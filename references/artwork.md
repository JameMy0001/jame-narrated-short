# Artwork and caption review

Generate native 9:16 (576×1024 by default) with no lettering. Place the main face and prop in the upper or middle portion. Reserve a low-detail caption area near `x=72–904, y=1320–1475` at 1080×1920. Open at full size and 360×640. Verify the caption overlay clears app controls at the right and bottom.

- Brief the specific subject, action, place, shot, lighting and palette. Check the character's age, proportions, wardrobe and props against the script. Inspect eyes, hands and joints, grip, layered silhouettes, shadows and light direction. Reject anatomy defects, blurred subjects, intersecting props, lettering, logos or watermarks.
- Inspect all four edges. Reject borders, embedded canvases, bars and transparency. Compare with other episodes and the closest previous composition. Different events, blocking, framing or lighting should be visible. Use previous work only as a text description of composition.
- Request one item per slot and record its actual result reference, tool and format. Resume with reviewed PNGs. For a failed visual check, log the visible defect, change the brief, and replace only the failing slot. Keep the prepared story and briefs if the generator is unavailable.
- Open the rendered captioned video with sound. Check tone marks, word breaks, cue timing, and the longest one- or two-line captions in context. Use `approve` after the final visual and playback check.
